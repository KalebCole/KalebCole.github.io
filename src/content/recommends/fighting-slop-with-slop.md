take: "This made me question how much of our software process is still designed around humans writing and reviewing every line. The TypeScript example stuck with me: a sound, provably correct type system is explicitly a non-goal, in favor of balancing correctness with productivity. That tradeoff made sense when humans wrote every line. It may be the wrong default when agents write most of the code. The answer is not blind trust in slop. It is layering determinism around it: architecture invariants, execution traces, tests, and type systems. Stop layering AI onto old processes and rebuild the foundations from first principles."
